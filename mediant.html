Overview

In Italian healthcare, documentation eats into visit time and turns booking a check-up into a chaotic experience. I designed MEDIANT, an AI-powered service with two connected touchpoints: a web platform that compiles visit files and flags anomalies in test results for doctors, and an app where patients find reports, next steps and voice help with medical terms.

Starting point

Keep the visit patient-centered, without sacrificing clinical accuracy.

Research on the Italian healthcare system revealed a recurring issue: doctors’ workflows are slowed down by time-consuming documentation, so visit time shifts from listening and dialogue to note-taking.

Desk research, brainwriting and in-depth interviews with doctors led to one question: how might we develop an AI-driven ecosystem that enables a deeper focus on patient and doctor communication during visits?

Outcome

A service concept and interface system made of two coordinated touchpoints.

  1. 01

    Doctor platform (web)

    a dashboard for appointments, patients and medical histories, with voice notes, AI-generated visit summaries and AI flagging anomalies in recent test results.

  2. 02

    Patient app (mobile)

    secure access to health information, appointment booking, health data monitoring, a next-steps guide after each visit and a voice assistant.

The two sides exchange information continuously: the doctor easily finds patient information, which the patient keeps updated through the app.

  1. Step 1

    Patient · app

    Keeps health data up to date and books visits.

  2. Step 2

    Mediant · AI core

    Organizes data sources and turns voice notes into documentation.

  3. Step 3

    Doctor · web platform

    Finds patient information and AI-generated visit summaries.

Key design decisions

The patient-centered visit as the core KPI.

  1. 01

    Real-time capture

    voice notes and AI-generated documentation reduce manual typing.

  2. 02

    One shared logic

    doctor and patient experiences built on the same data structure.

  3. 03

    Data sources as a constraint

    each feature designed around the availability and reliability of its data.

  4. 04

    Atomic design system

    consistent components across a complex platform.
